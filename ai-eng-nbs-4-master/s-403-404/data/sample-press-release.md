## TechVision Dynamics Announces Record Q3 Earnings and Strategic Partnership with GlobalServe Solutions

**NEW YORK, NY** - TechVision Dynamics (NASDAQ: TVD) today announced record-breaking third-quarter earnings, reporting $437.8 million in revenue for the period ending August 31, 2024, representing a 27.5% increase compared to the same period last year. The company also revealed a strategic partnership with GlobalServe Solutions, set to launch on November 12, 2024.

"Our exceptional Q3 performance reflects our team's dedication and our successful expansion into emerging markets," said Michael Chen, CEO of TechVision Dynamics. "The 27.5% year-over-year growth exceeds our projected target of 24% and positions us strongly for Q4 and beyond."

The company reported the following financial highlights:
- Q3 Revenue: $437,800,000 (€396.5 million)
- Net Income: $87.2 million, up from $62.5 million in Q3 2023
- Earnings Per Share: $3.42, exceeding analyst expectations of $2.98
- Operating Margin: 22.4% (+3.2% from previous quarter)
- R&D Investment: $76.3 million (17.4% of revenue)

TechVision's board has approved a quarterly dividend of $0.85 per share, payable on October 15, 2024, to shareholders of record as of September 30, 2024.

The strategic partnership with GlobalServe Solutions, valued at approximately €215 million ($237.3 million), will enhance TechVision's cloud infrastructure capabilities and expand its market presence in Europe and Asia. The multi-phase implementation will begin on 11/12/2024 and is expected to be completed by March 28, 2025.

"This partnership represents a significant milestone in our global strategy," said Elena Rodriguez, Chief Strategy Officer at TechVision. "By combining our AI solutions with GlobalServe's infrastructure, we estimate capturing an additional 8.7% market share in key regions by Q2 2025."

The company will host an investor call on September 20, 2024, at 2:30 PM EST. Participants can register by emailing investor.relations@techvisiondynamics.com or calling 1-800-555-1234.

TechVision reported strong performance across all regions:

| Region | Revenue (millions) | YoY Growth | Market Share |
|--------|-------------------|------------|--------------|
| North America | $198.5 | 22.3% | 34.2% |
| Europe | €126.8 ($140.1) | 31.7% | 27.8% |

The Asia-Pacific region showed particularly strong growth following the opening of TechVision's new Singapore office on April 15, 2024, which now employs 125 people.

## Product Developments

TechVision announced several product developments during Q3:

1. **NeuraCLoud 5.0** - Latest version of flagship AI platform launched on 07/22/2024, with 45% performance improvements
2. **DataSecure Pro** - Enhanced cybersecurity solution for enterprise clients, pricing starts at $12,500/month
3. **Vision Analytics Suite** - Business intelligence platform with 12 new features, to be released on 10/31/2024

"Our product pipeline remains robust, with R&D expenses of $76.3 million representing our commitment to innovation," said Dr. James Williams, CTO. "The team achieved a 23% reduction in development cycles while improving quality metrics across all product lines."

## Hiring and Expansion

TechVision added 178 employees globally in Q3, bringing the total workforce to 2,743. The company plans to hire an additional 200-250 employees by December 31, 2024, with 35% of new positions in engineering roles.

Construction of the company's new Austin campus at 12300 Technology Blvd, Austin, TX 78727 is 75% complete and on schedule for the January 15, 2025 opening. The 125,000-square-foot facility represents a $95 million investment and will accommodate up to 850 employees.

## Corporate Responsibility Initiatives

TechVision expanded its community impact programs in Q3:

- Launched the TechVision Foundation with an initial $12.5 million endowment
- Committed to reducing carbon emissions by 42% by 2030
- Partnered with 15 universities to provide $3.5 million in scholarships
- Achieved 100% renewable energy usage at 78% of facilities

"Our growth comes with responsibility to our communities and environment," said Carmen Diaz, Chief Sustainability Officer. "Our ESG initiatives remain central to our long-term strategy, with measurable goals and transparent reporting."
